The refined identity

Color palette

Ink#26352e

Sage#dce5d8

Use paper backgrounds, jade buttons, ink text, sage surfaces, and small clay accents. The original treatment scene uses jade, muted jade #557C69 and soft sage #8FAA98. Use the ivory logo on jade.

Typography

Use Cormorant Garamond for the name and DM Sans for the ACUPUNCTURE descriptor. Production SVG files contain outlined lettering; editable masters retain live type. Use the supplied lockups to preserve the spacing.

Space and scale

¼ symbol width

Leave at least one quarter of the symbol width clear around a logo. Recommended digital widths: symbol 48 px, horizontal logo 250 px, stacked logo 220 px. App icons use the supplied 32 px, 64 px or 180 px exports.

Keep the proportions and use the supplied colors. Preserve the clear gap below the name’s descenders; do not move the ACUPUNCTURE descriptor upward. Place the logo against a plain background with enough contrast to show the treatment scene.

Wallpapers & social backgrounds

Choose an orientation and background. Every wallpaper uses the same horizontal lockup, font and internal spacing as the approved ivory reference. The logo spans the same proportion of canvas width and sits at the center. The artwork contains no added slogan or decorative text.

Landscape downloads include 4K and 1080p. Portraits, stories and squares come at the sizes shown below. Outlined SVG files work without installed fonts; editable SVG masters use the included fonts.
