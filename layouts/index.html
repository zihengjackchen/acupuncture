Do I need to know which treatment to book? +

No. You can call to talk through your concerns and ask which appointment is a good place to begin. At Elite Healthcare, the booking page also lists available acupuncture appointments.

What if I’m new to acupuncture? +

You’re welcome here. Dan will explain the process, listen to your questions, and discuss your comfort before treatment. Let her know about medications, pregnancy, and other care you’re receiving.

How do I book at each location? +

Use Elite Healthcare’s booking page for Chicago appointments on Monday or Wednesday. For Sunday appointments in Highland Park, call 847-786-0066 to confirm the time.

Are home visits still available? +

Yes, mobile visits are available upon request. Use the ZIP lookup above to check the service area, then call to confirm your address, timing, and fees.
