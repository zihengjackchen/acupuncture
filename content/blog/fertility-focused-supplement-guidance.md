A brief virtual consultation costs **$20**. Mobile visits have a **base travel charge of $29.99 per commute**, separate from treatment fees.

For Chicago treatment fees, check [Elite Healthcare’s booking system](https://elitehcare.janeapp.com/). For Highland Park or a mobile visit, call [847-786-0066](tel:+18477860066) to confirm the treatment fee, appointment length, and total cost.

Ask about **package options and senior discounts**, and confirm insurance participation before booking.

Mobile visits are available by request. A ZIP match does not confirm an appointment or the travel charge for your address.
