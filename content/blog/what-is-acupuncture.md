During acupuncture, a practitioner inserts fine needles at selected points on the body. Dan reviews your history, explains the proposed treatment, and checks your comfort.

Evidence varies by condition, and people respond in different ways. Read the [NIH overview of effectiveness and safety](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/acupuncture-effectiveness-and-safety), then discuss your goals at a consultation.
