Acupuncture can cause soreness, minor bleeding, or bruising. Improper treatment can cause serious complications. Ask Dan about technique and safety practices before you begin.

Share your medications, pregnancy status, implanted devices, bleeding conditions, and relevant medical history so she can assess the proposed treatment.

Do not postpone medical evaluation for an acupuncture visit. [Read the NIH safety overview](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/acupuncture-effectiveness-and-safety) or [contact Dan](/contact/) with questions.
