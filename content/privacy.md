This website provides information about Wang’s Harmony Acupuncture and links to clinic appointment services. It does not include a medical intake form or patient account system.

## Your ZIP lookup

The mobile service-area lookup runs in your browser. The ZIP code you enter is checked against the service-area list on this website and is not submitted to a booking service or stored by this site.

## Calls and email

For general questions, call [847-786-0066](tel:+18477860066) or email [hello@wangsharmonyacu.com](mailto:hello@wangsharmonyacu.com). Please discuss sensitive health information during your appointment or through your clinic’s intake process rather than sending it in a general email.

## Appointment and directions links

The Chicago booking link takes you to Elite Healthcare’s Jane appointment system. Directions links open Google Maps. These services operate under their own privacy policies. Highland Park appointments are arranged by telephone.

## Website delivery

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses and access logs, to deliver and secure the website. This version of the website does not include analytics or advertising trackers.
