Tell Dan about your pain, any medical evaluation, and the activities you want to return to. She will discuss whether acupuncture fits alongside your current care and review your response at follow-up.

Research suggests acupuncture may help some back, neck, knee pain, and headache conditions. Results vary. [Read the NIH overview](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/acupuncture-effectiveness-and-safety).

Seek medical assessment for a new injury, unexplained pain, or worsening symptoms before an acupuncture visit.
