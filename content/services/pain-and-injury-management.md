Pain can change the way you move, rest, and spend your day. We begin by understanding the symptoms, any medical evaluation you have had, and the activities you want to return to.

## A plan you can review

Dan will discuss whether acupuncture is appropriate alongside your existing care. Goals might include everyday comfort, movement, or the ability to take part in the routines that matter to you. Your response guides follow-up.

Research suggests acupuncture may help some back, neck, knee pain, and headache conditions. Benefits vary by condition and person. [Read the NIH overview of acupuncture research](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/acupuncture-effectiveness-and-safety).

New injuries, unexplained pain, or worsening symptoms need medical assessment. Acupuncture should not delay that evaluation.
