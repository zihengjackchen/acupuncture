Discuss your symptoms and existing care with Dan. If you are trying to conceive or receiving IUI or IVF, bring your treatment schedule and your fertility team’s recommendations.

In studies summarized by [the NIH](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/acupuncture-effectiveness-and-safety), researchers found no difference in IVF pregnancy or live-birth rates between acupuncture and sham acupuncture. We do not promise pregnancy or improved egg quality.

Tell Dan if you are pregnant or may be pregnant. Review proposed herbs or supplements with your medical care team before use.
