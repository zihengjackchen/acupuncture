WALLPAPERS
Three color sets: ivory (jade logo), jade (ivory logo), sage (jade logo).
Landscape 16:9: 3840x2160 and 1920x1080 PNG.
Portrait 3:4: 1440x1920 PNG.
Portrait 4:5: 1080x1350 PNG.
Story / phone 9:16: 1080x1920 PNG.
Square 1:1: 1080x1080 PNG.

Filename pattern: wallpapers/{color}-{orientation}-{ratio}-{width}x{height}.png
Examples: wallpapers/ivory-landscape-16x9-3840x2160.png and wallpapers/sage-story-9x16-1080x1920.png.
Outlined vectors: wallpapers/{color}-{orientation}-{ratio}.svg
Live-type masters: editable/wallpapers/{color}-{orientation}-{ratio}.svg
All formats use the approved horizontal lockup, with identical typography and internal spacing. The logo spans 2200/3840 (57.2917%) of canvas width and is centered by visible artwork bounds. The reference is development/brand/options/g-wallpaper-ivory.svg.

The older wallpaper-*.png and wallpaper-jade-*.png filenames remain available.
